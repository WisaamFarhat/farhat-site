IFIC 3065

COMMENT DEADLINE17 June 2026

BR IFIC 3065, published 17 February 2026, carries 5 coordination requests, of which 3 are GSO and 2 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include TIBASAT-G2-40.5E (EGY, GSO, 40.5°E); ASIASAT-100.5N (CHN, GSO, 100.5°E); INSAT-NAV-MEO (IND, NGSO). Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (16), 9.12A (21), 9.13 (85). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 17 June 2026.
