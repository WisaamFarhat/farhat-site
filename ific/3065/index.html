BR IFIC   17 February 2026

IFIC 3065

COMMENT DEADLINE17 June 2026

BR IFIC 3065, published 17 February 2026, carries 49 coordination requests, of which 44 are GSO and 5 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include INSAT-NAV-MEO (IND, NGSO); TIBASAT-G2-40.5E (EGY, GSO, 40.5°E); TIANQI-3E (CHN, NGSO). Notably, CHN filed a cluster of 38 networks in the CHNSAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (154), 9.12A (600), 9.13 (6832). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 17 June 2026.
