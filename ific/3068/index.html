IFIC 3068

COMMENT DEADLINE31 July 2026

BR IFIC 3068, published 31 March 2026, carries 26 coordination requests, of which 14 are GSO and 12 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include KOREASAT-NGSO-K-M (KOR, NGSO); LM-BLUESKYNEXUS (CYP, NGSO); CSN-SSO (CHN, NGSO). Notably, QAT filed a cluster of 3 networks in the QATARSAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (1342), 9.12A (4973), 9.13 (1836). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 31 July 2026.
