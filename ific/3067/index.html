BR IFIC   17 March 2026

IFIC 3067

COMMENT DEADLINE17 July 2026

BR IFIC 3067, published 17 March 2026, carries 19 coordination requests, of which 14 are GSO and 5 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include OMANSAT-54.5E-1 (OMA, GSO, 54.5°E); OMANSAT-72E-1 (OMA, GSO, 72.0°E); SSG-CSL (G, NGSO). Notably, G filed a cluster of 7 networks in the INMARSAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (223), 9.12A (215), 9.13 (2684). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 17 July 2026.
