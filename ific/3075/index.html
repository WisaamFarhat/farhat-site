BR IFIC   7 July 2026

IFIC 3075

COMMENT DEADLINE7 November 2026

BR IFIC 3075, published 7 July 2026, carries 29 coordination requests, of which 19 are GSO and 10 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include BISTROMATH (UAE, NGSO); STARARC (CHN, NGSO); JARVIS-2 (LUX, NGSO). Notably, LUX filed a cluster of 9 networks in the LUX family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (957), 9.12A (3348), 9.13 (3589). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 7 November 2026.
