BR IFIC   26 May 2026

IFIC 3072

COMMENT DEADLINE26 September 2026

BR IFIC 3072, published 26 May 2026, carries 30 coordination requests, of which 11 are GSO and 19 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include VISTA-1 (G, NGSO); KOREASAT-MEO-K (KOR, NGSO); GALAXY-5 (CHN, NGSO). Notably, USA filed a cluster of 3 networks in the USASAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (1738), 9.12A (7177), 9.13 (566). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 26 September 2026.
