IFIC 3073

COMMENT DEADLINE9 October 2026

BR IFIC 3073, published 9 June 2026, carries 27 coordination requests, of which 18 are GSO and 9 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include CANARYSAT (G, NGSO); PCL-PEGASUS (CHN, NGSO); INSAT-NAV-NGSO-MS25 (IND, NGSO). Notably, F filed a cluster of 7 networks in the F family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (638), 9.12A (2916), 9.13 (3290). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 9 October 2026.
