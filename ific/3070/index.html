BR IFIC   28 April 2026

IFIC 3070

COMMENT DEADLINE28 August 2026

BR IFIC 3070, published 28 April 2026, carries 22 coordination requests, of which 14 are GSO and 8 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include TERRESTAR-NGSO-1 (CAN, NGSO); RASSVET-3 (RUS, NGSO); JIUCHEN (CHN, NGSO). Notably, E filed a cluster of 4 networks in the HISPASAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (1204), 9.12A (5949), 9.13 (2342). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 28 August 2026.
