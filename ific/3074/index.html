BR IFIC   23 June 2026

IFIC 3074

COMMENT DEADLINE23 October 2026

BR IFIC 3074, published 23 June 2026, carries 40 coordination requests, of which 19 are GSO and 21 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include USASAT-NGSO-8F (USA, NGSO); USASAT-NGSO-8E (USA, NGSO); STARERA-3 (CHN, NGSO). Notably, USA filed a cluster of 6 networks in the USASAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (2105), 9.12A (8594), 9.13 (1739). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 23 October 2026.
