BR IFIC   12 May 2026

IFIC 3071

COMMENT DEADLINE12 September 2026

BR IFIC 3071, published 12 May 2026, carries 24 coordination requests, of which 17 are GSO and 7 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include ARABSAT-12Y-9W (ARS, GSO, 9.0°W); ARABSAT-12N-14.7E (ARS, GSO, 14.7°E); ARABSAT-12G-1E (ARS, GSO, 1.0°E). Notably, ARS filed a cluster of 6 networks in the ARABSAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (261), 9.13 (3156). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 12 September 2026.
