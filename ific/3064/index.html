BR IFIC   3 February 2026

IFIC 3064

COMMENT DEADLINE3 June 2026

BR IFIC 3064, published 3 February 2026, carries 30 coordination requests, of which 21 are GSO and 9 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include TELSTAR-LEO-G1 (CAN, NGSO); HISPASAT-LEO-NB-4 (E, NGSO); FGN-100-D1 (TUR, NGSO). Notably, USA filed a cluster of 6 networks in the USBGB family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (502), 9.12A (2004), 9.13 (1810). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 3 June 2026.
