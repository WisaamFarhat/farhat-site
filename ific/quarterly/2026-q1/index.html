QUARTERLY SUMMARY   6 January to 31 March 2026

First Quarter 2026

Every BR IFIC of the quarter, aggregated: the filings, the administrations behind them, and the networks with the broadest coordination reach.

Across 7 biweekly circulars, IFIC 3062 through 3068, the Bureau published 225 coordination requests this quarter, an average of 32.1 per issue. Of these, 166 are GSO and 59 NGSO. Filings came from 39 administrations, led by China with 77 requests, 34 percent of the quarter. Coordination this quarter is invoked most often under 9.13 (21911), 9.12A (14384), 9.12 (3984).
