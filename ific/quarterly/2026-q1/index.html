Quarterly Review   IFIC 3062 to 3068

First Quarter 2026

6 January to 31 March 2026. The quarter's BR IFIC coordination activity, aggregated across every circular.

Across Q1 2026, the Bureau published 7 space services circulars carrying 225 coordination requests, 166 GSO and 59 NGSO. China was the single largest source with 77 filings. Coordination was invoked most often under 9.13 (21,911), 9.12A (14,384), 9.12 (3,984). The 40,279 coordination pairings are dominated by a handful of large NGSO constellation filings, so the typical filing reached a median of 168 existing networks.
