Quarterly Review   IFIC 3069 to 3074

Second Quarter 2026

14 April to 23 June 2026. The quarter's BR IFIC coordination activity, aggregated across every circular.

Across Q2 2026, the Bureau published 6 space services circulars carrying 202 coordination requests, 125 GSO and 77 NGSO. China was the single largest source with 43 filings. Coordination was invoked most often under 9.12A (26,964), 9.13 (14,040), 9.12 (6,804). The 47,808 coordination pairings are dominated by a handful of large NGSO constellation filings, so the typical filing reached a median of 197 existing networks.
