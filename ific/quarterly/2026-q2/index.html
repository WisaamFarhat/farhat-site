QUARTERLY SUMMARY   14 April to 23 June 2026

Second Quarter 2026

Every BR IFIC of the quarter, aggregated: the filings, the administrations behind them, and the networks with the broadest coordination reach.

Across 6 biweekly circulars, IFIC 3069 through 3074, the Bureau published 202 coordination requests this quarter, an average of 33.7 per issue. Of these, 125 are GSO and 77 NGSO. Filings came from 30 administrations, led by China with 43 requests, 21 percent of the quarter. Coordination this quarter is invoked most often under 9.12A (26964), 9.13 (14040), 9.12 (6804).
