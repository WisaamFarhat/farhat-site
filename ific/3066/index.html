IFIC 3066

COMMENT DEADLINE   3 July 2026   -3 days left

BR IFIC 3066, published 3 March 2026, carries 14 coordination requests, of which 12 are GSO and 2 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include LUX-73E (LUX, GSO, 73.0°E); MADAR-118.7W (UAE, GSO, 118.7°W); MADAR-106E (UAE, GSO, 106.0°E). Notably, LUX filed a cluster of 5 networks in the LUX family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (36), 9.13 (456). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 3 July 2026.
