BR IFIC   3 March 2026

IFIC 3066

COMMENT DEADLINE3 July 2026

BR IFIC 3066, published 3 March 2026, carries 61 coordination requests, of which 52 are GSO and 9 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include CSN-MEO (CHN, NGSO); GW-A59 (CHN, NGSO); SUPA-NGSO-1 (D, NGSO). Notably, CHN filed a cluster of 12 networks in the CHNNEWSAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (792), 9.12A (3335), 9.13 (6204). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 3 July 2026.
