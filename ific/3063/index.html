IFIC 3063

COMMENT DEADLINE20 May 2026

BR IFIC 3063, published 20 January 2026, carries 35 coordination requests, of which 20 are GSO and 15 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include PANDA (CHN, NGSO); USASAT-NGSO-20 (USA, NGSO); BLACKSPIDER-MSS-2 (CHN, NGSO). Notably, USA filed a cluster of 4 networks in the USASAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (802), 9.12A (2894), 9.13 (2545). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 20 May 2026.
