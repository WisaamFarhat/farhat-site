IFIC 3069

COMMENT DEADLINE14 August 2026

BR IFIC 3069, published 14 April 2026, carries 59 coordination requests, of which 46 are GSO and 13 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include HISPASAT-LEO-BB-3 (E, NGSO); LUX-N1-2 (LUX, NGSO); AUSSAT-I-156E (AUS, GSO, 156.0°E). Notably, J filed a cluster of 11 networks in the N family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (858), 9.12A (2328), 9.13 (2947). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 14 August 2026.
