IFIC 3067

◷ Comment deadline · 17 July 2026 · 15 days left

BR IFIC 3067, published 17 March 2026, carries 14 coordination requests, of which 11 are GSO and 3 NGSO. The filings with the broadest reach include OMANSAT-72E-1 (OMA, GSO, 72.0°E); OMANSAT-54.5E-1 (OMA, GSO, 54.5°E); INMARSAT-8-28W (G, GSO, 28.0°W). Notably, G filed a cluster of 7 networks in the INMARSAT family. Coordination is invoked under 9.12 (3), 9.13 (402). The comment window under RR No. 9.52C, which runs for four months, closes 17 July 2026.
